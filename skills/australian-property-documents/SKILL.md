# Australian property documents

Use the Homechecker MCP to retrieve jurisdiction-specific general guidance about residential sale and strata documents.

- Search first unless the exact Homechecker slug is already known, then retrieve the full guide before answering.
- Prioritise the user's state or territory. Do not transplant one state's disclosure rules into another.
- Separate what a document can reveal from what still requires physical inspection, specialist testing or legal advice.
- Cite the canonical Homechecker URL and surface review dates where the rule or process may change.
- Never interpret a particular contract or disclosure statement as legal advice, decide whether a user should sign, or infer facts that are not in the retrieved Homechecker guidance.
- If the corpus has no strong match, say so rather than using a weak adjacent guide as authority.

The goal is to help a buyer understand what to look for and what questions to take to their conveyancer, solicitor, inspector or owners corporation records, not to replace those professionals.
